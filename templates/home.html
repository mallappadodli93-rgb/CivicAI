How CivicAI Works

Transparent 4-stage municipal workflow from citizen alert to verified resolution

1. Report Issue

Citizens describe the problem, specify locality, and attach optional photographic evidence from desktop or mobile.

2. AI Real-time Triage

Our NLP engine analyzes severity (1-100 score), eliminates duplicates, categorizes the grievance, and creates an executive briefing.

3. Field Routing

Automatically assigned to municipal departments (Power, Water, Roads, Sanitation, Safety) with officer deployment.

4. Verified Resolution

Citizens follow progress through 5 clear stages with a chronological audit trail from submission to resolution.
